## Canonical sentences

0. **Missionism designs companies so rational self-interest of contributor, company, and customer points the same way.** (Constitution v1.0)  
1. **We’re humans first, and we’re in this together.** (The Human Compact)  
2. **Every human being has equal standing in Missionism.**  
3. **People aren’t the problem. Problems are the problem.** / **Fix the system, not the human.**  
4. **Accountability asks what must change. Blame asks whom we should hate.**  
5. **Attack problems. Challenge ideas. Never make people the enemy.**  
6. **Make the mission true. Own what you make true.**  
7. **Don’t fight over the pie until you’ve tried to make the pie bigger.**  
8. **Missionism defines what is possible. Mishys makes discovery useful — and never sells the ranking.**  
9. **Don’t start from scratch. Start from what works.**  
10. **Mission determines what we subsidize. Value determines what we charge.**  
11. **Never make the mission more expensive simply because the mission is succeeding.**  
12. **Optimize the business to fund the Mission. Optimize the Mission to decide what businesses should exist.**  
13. **Products are hypotheses. The Mission is durable. The human floor isn’t forkable.**  
14. **Maximize expected change in decision quality—not answer polish.**  
15. **Search for high-value, low-structure human/world signal lost to expensive translation.**  
16. **A paycheck pays for today. Ownership should help pay for tomorrow.**  
17. **The people who spend their lives creating long-term value should steadily become owners of that value.**  
18. **The goal is not to make employees dependent on the company. The goal is to make their years of contribution create more independence.**  
19. **Capital can buy economics. It cannot simply buy the mission.**  
20. **Private is the destination; public is the escape hatch. Never treat IPO as graduation.**  
21. **Missionism is not anti-founder. It is anti-permanent entitlement to future value that other people create.**  
22. **Time makes equity available. Mission progress unlocks it. Contribution earns it.**  
23. **The closer something is to the Mission, the harder it should be to change.**  
24. **People do not belong to Missions. Their contributions do.**  
25. **Choosing cash never means you believe in the Mission less.**  
26. **Believe in people. Do not depend on kings.**  
27. **Think for 100 years. Experiment this week.**  
28. **The Mission is the score. Capital is a tool.**  
29. **Good Mechanism ⇒ Human ↑ · Company ↑ · Customer ↑ · Mission ↑**  
30. **Improve tomorrow’s ruler. Do not rewrite yesterday’s ruler.**  
31. **Can we redesign so the loser stops losing?**  
34. **Create $5. Keep $1. Make everyone richer.** (Era bargain)  
35. **You can’t mine yesterday’s value. You can own part of creating tomorrow’s.**  
36. **Dilution is justified when someone created the new value that made everyone else’s shares worth more.**  
37. **Mine as fast as the weaker of mission progress and financial progress.**
