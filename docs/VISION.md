# Vision

Updated: 2026-10-03

## Why this exists

Build an open-source future for the 99%, by the 99%.

Anyone should be able to identify something that should exist, start a Mission, find collaborators, contribute, earn recognized ownership, participate in decisions, and benefit from what they help build.

99pct removes the administrative friction between wanting to help and actually helping.

The locked public definition of Missionism stays in the application at `src/content/voice.ts` (`shortDefinition`). Protocol purpose and human constraints stay in `spec/canonical.json` (`claims.purpose`, `humanConstraints`). This file does not restate them as a second normative source.

## What 99pct.com is for

99pct is the open-source operating system for building things together.

The homepage should let a person start something or find something worth building. The first action is a single prompt: what should exist? The system turns that into a draft Mission and asks only the questions that require human judgment.

A person should be able to help without first understanding corporate law. Behind a join action, 99pct coordinates agreements, evidence, contribution rules, and later ownership. The human sees what they are agreeing to.

## What success looks like

A visitor can go from an idea to people, an agreement, a recorded contribution, recognized Mission Contribution Units, and eventually ownership and impact.

Money finances a Mission. Work owns the Mission. Capital may arrive as revenue, preorders, customer commitments, grants, loans, or other non-equity mechanisms. Buying the ownership that contributors earn is a separate, regulated rail, introduced later if at all.

## What this file does not decide

Citizenship rules, tax forms, securities exemptions, equity math, and industry-specific control tests are compliance outputs for a particular Mission, jurisdiction, industry, and person. They are not a global "Americans only" or "collect a Social Security number at signup" rule.

Legal conclusions in chat are not counsel. Securities, tax, and employment design wait for counsel and a later work order.
