## Product thesis

Missionism ownership should eventually be economically useful **without requiring the owner to sell it**.

```text
Do the work. Earn ownership.
And ownership isn't something you have to wait 30 years to benefit from.
```

Public framing (lead with utility, not leverage):

> **Your ownership should be useful before you sell it.**  
> **Own it. Keep it. Use it.**

Do **not** lead with: borrow against your shares · Missionism bank · leverage · financial engineering · recursive investment · 5:1.

## Where it sits in the architecture

```text
Missionism                         protocol / ownership laws
    ↓
Mishys                             compiler / organizational OS
    ↓
MU / economic truth                contribution → ownership state
    ↓
Standard Missionism security       legible ownership asset (settlement)
    ↓
Ownership Liquidity / Capital rail makes eligible ownership financeable
    ↓
Banks / credit unions / private credit / other regulated partners
                                   provide actual financing
```

| Layer | Role |
|-------|------|
| **MU** | Economic truth of contribution — never a loan balance |
| **Securities adapter** | Legal share / unit settlement — may be designed *pledgeable* |
| **Succession** | Keep ownership across exit/generations — liquidity complements, does not replace |
| **Ownership account** | Contributor-facing record of stake |
| **Use your ownership** | Future capability under Ownership — not a peer of formation |
| **Capital partners** | Independent underwriting and cash — **not** the operating company |

**Mishys is not the lender.** Mishys standardizes ownership so external institutions can understand and potentially finance it.

Separation that must never collapse:

```text
MISSIONISM OWNERSHIP SYSTEM     creates and tracks legitimate ownership
MISSION CAPITAL SYSTEM          makes some ownership financially useful
```

## System laws

### Core law

> **Ownership may create borrowing capacity. Borrowing must never create ownership value.**

### Product laws

```text
VALUE_PRECEDES_CREDIT
Real economic value must exist before credit can be created against ownership.

CREDIT_DOES_NOT_DEFINE_VALUE
A loan secured by Missionism ownership cannot itself be evidence that
the ownership has increased in value.

ONE_ASSET_ONE_PLEDGE
The same ownership interest cannot simultaneously collateralize multiple
obligations unless explicitly structured and disclosed.

INDEPENDENT_UNDERWRITING
Borrowing capacity must be determined independently of the operating
company’s desire to increase its valuation.
The operating company must not manufacture liquidity by lending against
its own self-declared valuation.

ZERO_GROWTH_SURVIVABILITY
If for five years there are zero new Missionism companies, zero new
contributors, zero valuation increases, and zero new outside equity —
every existing loan must still be serviceable or recoverable from
borrower income, company cash flows, collateral, and reserves.
If today’s obligations require tomorrow’s entrants → structurally invalid.

OWNERSHIP_WITHOUT_FORCED_EXIT
Prefer allowing contributors to retain productive ownership rather than
requiring a sale merely to make that wealth useful.
```

### Near-term product constraint (v1 capital rail)

Borrowed proceeds for **non-purpose** uses only (home, education, major purchase, emergency, starting a real business — subject to partner policy).

Purchasing Missionism (or other) securities with Mission Credit is **out of scope for v1** — separate product later, if ever.

Recursive “borrow → buy Missionism equity → borrow again” is **not allowed** in the conceptual v1 design.

## What Mishys builds near-term vs later

### Near-term (architecture + standards)

Create a **standardized private-company ownership instrument and data model** that *could* become financeable:

- ownership records · vesting · transfer restrictions  
- default / lien treatment · dilution · voting  
- company information rights · secondary liquidity hooks  
- pledge eligibility flag (prospective policy)

### Explicitly not now

No lending, valuation engines, credit scoring, payments, underwriting APIs, or fund formation.

Illustrative UI may show **mocked** lendable values clearly labeled as future / not an offer.

### Later (adapters, not Missionism Core)

1. Ownership standard  
2. Shares designed to be pledgeable  
3. Partner lender provides credit  
4. Diversified Mission Credit pool (many companies)  
5. Possible cooperative financial institution — **optional distant future**

Correlation risk (employer stock + salary + collateral in one company) requires **more conservative** treatment than diversified public portfolios — not less.

## Healthy vs unhealthy loops

Healthy:

> productive enterprise → equity → collateral → capital → productive enterprise

Unhealthy (forbidden):

> equity → loans → higher equity price → bigger loans → higher equity price

Mondragon’s Caja Laboral is a **historical cousin** (worker ownership needed a financial system around it) — not a template to copy into Canonical Missionism.
