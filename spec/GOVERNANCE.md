## Separation of rights

Overlap, not identity:

- **Economic rights** (MCU / ownership participation)  
- **Operational authority** (running Mines and teams)  
- **Constitutional governance** (Mission meaning, Contract, amendments)  

Historical MCU accumulation must not automatically control what “the Mission” means.

**Wealth can be inherited. Permanent political control should not automatically be inherited.** Outside capital may hold Capital Shares (economic participation) without buying Contributor governance. Capital can buy economics; it cannot simply buy the mission. See [OWNERSHIP.md](./OWNERSHIP.md) and `docs/OWNERSHIP_AND_CAPITAL_SYSTEM.md`.

## Mission vs Enterprise (anti-capture)

Organizations under a Mission maintain separate Mission Value and Enterprise Value equations, a Strategic Portfolio (including Disrupt Us), and Mission Reinvestment. Products are hypotheses—not constitutionally protected. See [ORGANIZATION.md](./ORGANIZATION.md).

## Amendments = proposals, not popularity contests

Prefer pull-request style changes:

Proposed change → Reasoning → Evidence → Predicted effect → Discussion → Review → Merge / Reject → History preserved.

👍/👎 scoreboards are not governance.

## Fork right

Anyone may fork a Mission’s reasoning artifacts. Competing versions compete for people, capital, results, and credibility. Voice + exit + fork is more robust than voting alone.

## Sunset

Missions periodically ask whether they still need to exist: Continue · Redefine · Merge · Split · Mission accomplished · Terminate.
