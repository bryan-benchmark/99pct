# The Human Compact

**Sits beneath Missionism itself**—not as one Mission among many.

**Authority:** The five non-negotiable human constraints are originated in
[`canonical.json`](./canonical.json) (`humanConstraints`, HC-01…HC-05), under
umbrella claim `humansNotVariables`. **This file explains, interprets, and
illustrates them. It does not originate them.**

Missionism can be radically pluralistic about *what* people want to improve, while being non-pluralistic about the *basic conditions* required for people to work together.

**Premise:** We’re humans first, and we’re in this together.

Before we’re members of different Missions, tribes, religions, countries, companies, or ideologies—we’re humans sharing the same system.

---

## Standing sentence

Every human being has equal standing in Missionism.

We can disagree about ideas, priorities, beliefs, methods, and Missions. We cannot deny one another’s humanity.

---

## Core law (lean generalized)

**People aren’t the problem. Problems are the problem.**  
**Fix the system, not the human.**

Lean manufacturing: when something goes wrong, improve the system rather than searching for someone to blame. Missionism generalizes that from organizations to society—stronger than “be nice.”

| Mission isn’t against… | Mission is against… |
|------------------------|---------------------|
| Homeowners / landlords | Conditions producing homelessness |
| Doctors / insurers / patients / admins | Mechanisms producing poor healthcare outcomes |
| A race or class of people | Behaviors, incentives, beliefs, institutions, processes that produce unequal treatment |

Even when an individual behaves terribly: hold them accountable without making them the Mission’s enemy. Ask: What behavior occurred? What caused or enabled it? What consequences protect others? What system change makes recurrence less likely?

```
Human dignity → no enemies → diagnose causes → improve systems → measure outcomes → iterate
```

**Accountability asks what must change. Blame asks whom we should hate.**  
Missionism needs the first and has very little use for the second.

“Don’t blame the person” does **not** mean “nobody is responsible.” People still have agency. Boundaries still exist. Violence can require intervention. Harmful behavior can have consequences.

Every person has equal human worth. People will make mistakes, hold bad ideas, and sometimes cause harm. Hold behavior accountable, but don’t confuse a human being with the problem you’re trying to solve. Look upstream: incentives, environments, information, processes, institutions, and opportunities. Improve the conditions that produce behavior, and measure whether people’s lives actually improve.

---

## What this is not

Not a long ideological checklist (anti-X membership list). That accidentally makes Missionism feel like a particular political culture.

The standard is **behavior toward other humans**, not conformity of thought. Belonging does not require adopting any founder’s politics, vocabulary, culture, or worldview.

---

## Five invariants

These correspond to HC-01…HC-05 in [`canonical.json`](./canonical.json). Wording below is explanatory; the machine-readable norms are the JSON strings.

1. **Equal human worth** (HC-01 `equalStanding`)  
   Race, sex, age, nationality, disability, religion, sexuality, wealth, status, and similar categories do not determine someone’s human value.

2. **Freedom of conscience** (HC-02 `freedomOfConscience`)  
   Believe, worship, question, speak, disagree, and change your mind—while protecting that freedom for everyone else.

3. **Nonviolence and non-coercion** (HC-03 `nonCoercion`)  
   Missions improve human conditions. They do not achieve ends through violence, intimidation, or domination.

4. **Ideas are challengeable; people retain dignity** (HC-04 `humanDignity`)  
   No belief or Mission is above criticism. Disagreement is not permission to dehumanize. Attack problems. Challenge ideas. Compete over solutions. Never make people the enemy.

5. **Reciprocity** (HC-05 `reciprocity`)  
   The freedoms, dignity, and protections you claim for yourself must also be granted to people you profoundly disagree with.

---

## Pluralism that follows

A Christian Mission can exist. An atheist Mission can exist. A conservative Mission can exist. A progressive Mission can exist. They can argue vigorously.

But nobody gets to deny equal standing, coerce belief, strip reciprocity—or redefine a Mission as hatred of a class of people.
