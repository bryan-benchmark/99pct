# Globals

The physics of Missionism. Mechanisms (MCUs, Cells, Councils, products) may change. Globals should still make sense in 2126.

**Beneath the Globals:** [The Human Compact](./HUMAN_COMPACT.md)—equal standing, conscience, nonviolence, dignity in disagreement, reciprocity. Plural Missions; non-forkable human floor.

1. **Mission** — organize around a durable desired world state  
2. **Truth** — prefer evidence over narrative  
3. **Positive Sum** — grow the pie before dividing it  
4. **Collaboration** — seek mutual advancement before adversarial tradeoff  
5. **Contribution** — those who make outcomes true participate in value created  
6. **Long Term** — prefer durable progress over short extraction  
7. **Autonomy** — push decisions to the smallest competent unit  
8. **Transparency** — inspectable reasoning and history  
9. **Proof** — claims of progress require evidence  
10. **Stewardship** — no one may redefine winning, certify their own success, and award themselves the pie  
11. **Do No Hidden Harm** — never export costs invisibly  
12. **Evolution** — the protocol itself may improve under constrained process  

**Conflict rule:** when Globals conflict, prefer the interpretation that preserves Mission integrity, Truth, and Do No Hidden Harm.
