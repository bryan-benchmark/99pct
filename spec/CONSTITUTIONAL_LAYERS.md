# Constitutional Layers & Three Keys

**Universal Missionism governance skeleton.**  
Company-specific numbers (e.g. GoPrivate 50/50) live in that company’s constitution — not here.

Companion: [missions/GOPRIVATE_MISSIONISM_V0.1.md](./missions/GOPRIVATE_MISSIONISM_V0.1.md) · [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md) · [FOUNDER_CLOCK.md](./FOUNDER_CLOCK.md)

## Heart of the constitution

> **Doers + Builders + Mission Leader.**  
> **Any two keys can change the future.**  
> **Nobody can change the past.**

---

## Three keys

| Key | Represents |
|-----|------------|
| **Doer House** | Mission-production / outcome side |
| **Builder House** | People building the organization / system |
| **Mission Leader** | Long-term mission integrity |

For **Reserved Mission Matters** (constitutional / mission-definition class):

| Coalition | Result |
|-----------|--------|
| Doers + Builders | Passes — Mission Leader cannot stop |
| Doers + Mission Leader | Passes |
| Builders + Mission Leader | Passes |
| Doers vs Builders | Mission Leader decides |

Mission Leader is the permanent **office** that bridges the economic constituencies — not a shadow CEO, and not a permanent personal entitlement.

---

## Four layers of law

### Layer 1 — Immutable Kernel

Nobody changes these — not founder, not Mission Leader, not 100% of employees:

- Equity mining never stops  
- Closed MCUs cannot be rewritten  
- Earned shares cannot be confiscated through Missionism  
- A pivot cannot restart the founder clock  
- A successor company cannot reset Missionism history  
- Past eras cannot be retroactively altered  
- Future contributors must always have a path to ownership  

### Layer 2 — Company Constitution

Hard to change; changeable prospectively by **2 of 3 keys**.

Examples: mission statement, boring/fast half-lives, Mission Participation Split (doer/builder %), Mission Leader / House definitions, distribution / capital policy.

### Layer 3 — Era Charter

Changes each era. Locked once the era starts.

Examples: mission-unit equation, velocity curve / targets, Impact contracts, MCU assumptions, evidence standards.

### Layer 4 — Operations

CEO discretion. Changeable tomorrow morning. Keeps Missionism from becoming bureaucracy.

---

## Always a next era

At start of Era N, Missionism auto-creates **Default Era N+1** (carry forward equation, incremental target, participation split, MCU rules, capital policy).

- During Era N: Mission Leader may propose a better Era N+1 anytime  
- ≥90 days before end: proposed Era N+1 must be public internally  
- ≥30 days before end: locks if 2-of-3 approve  
- If nobody agrees: **Default Era N+1 starts automatically**  

No gap. No paused mining. No hostage negotiation.

> Failure to make a new law leaves the existing law in place.

Current era rules stay immutable for the duration of that era.

## Mission Participation Split (not universal 50/50)

Each company declares a doer/builder split that reflects **where causal work happens**, not moral ranking.

| Mission type (examples) | Possible split |
|-------------------------|----------------:|
| Labor-intensive care network | 80 / 20 |
| Marketplace (e.g. GoPrivate) | 50 / 50 |
| Education marketplace | 50 / 50 |
| Deep-tech R&D | 20 / 80 |
| Creator marketplace | 70 / 30 |

Unilateral founder or House capture of the split is forbidden; change requires 2 of 3, prospective only.

---

## Mission Leader office vs person

| Permanent | Not permanent by right |
|-----------|-------------------------|
| The office | The human holding it |
| Continuity + succession rules | Founder entitlement after special control ends |

Acting Mission Leader on incapacity: CEO runs ops; era/mining continue; no major constitutional change until Houses ratify.

---

## CEO vs Mission Leader

| Mission Leader | CEO |
|----------------|-----|
| Mission, equation, eras, participation split, constitutional integrity, mission drift | Hiring, firing, P&L, pricing, product, sales, budget, execution |

One human may hold both initially. They need not forever.

## Deeper invariant

> **If I help the mission more, the system should create a path for me to get more** — more MCUs, shares, participation, status, opportunity — even as percentages dilute when new contributors join.

And success is measured as:

> **Mission value per fully loaded builder resource** — not empire headcount.
