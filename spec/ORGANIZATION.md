# Organization under Mission

Organizations are economic engines that **fund and execute** Missions. They are not the Mission.

## Canonical tension

> Optimize the business to fund the Mission.  
> Optimize the Mission to decide what businesses should exist.

## Separate equations (constitutional)

| Equation | Owns |
|----------|------|
| **Mission Value** | Solution-agnostic better-world progress |
| **Enterprise Value** | Customer value + economic strength of the organization |
| **Customer / Product Value** | Local product economics, subject to Mission guardrails |
| **Option Value** | Expansion of future Mission potential and enterprise optionality |

Do not collapse these into one score. Score every product/venture: Mission · Enterprise · Option. Portfolio 2×2: Core engine · Cash engine (justify) · R&D/public-good · Kill.

## Required artifacts

- Enterprise Value Equation  
- Strategic Portfolio (including **Disrupt Us**)  
- Mission Reinvestment policy (Core Growth · Mission R&D · Open Infrastructure · Resilience)  
- Per product: Customer Value Equation + **causal Mission bridge**  
- Mines typed: Enterprise · Mission · Bridge (plus Outcome / Learning / Unlock)

## Anti-capture

1. No product, service, revenue stream, customer segment, or business model is constitutionally protected. Products are hypotheses; the Mission is durable.  
2. A materially superior path to the Mission must be allowed to compete with and replace existing products—even when that reduces revenue.  
3. Disrupt Us work may earn Mission MCUs even when it harms current product revenue.
