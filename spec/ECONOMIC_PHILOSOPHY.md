# Dual MCU & Pay Locally, Own Globally

Canonical detail: [`missionism-protocol/ECONOMIC_PHILOSOPHY.md`](../../missionism-protocol/ECONOMIC_PHILOSOPHY.md)

## Standing rules

> **Standardize the accounting of human contribution; do not standardize the outcomes of the missions they choose.**

> **Pay locally. Own globally.**  
> Geography can affect cash. Geography by itself cannot discount ownership credit for equal verified contribution.

## Dual meaning (same unit)

1. **Contribution accounting** — standardized credited contribution (Reference Human-Year = 10,000 MCU).  
2. **Ownership accounting** — fraction of a specific Mission pie only.

Equal contribution credit ≠ equal wealth. No global MCU market. No universal human merit score from lifetime MCU.
