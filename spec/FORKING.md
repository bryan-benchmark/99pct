# Forking

**Missionism Protocol v0.1 · Draft · September 2026**

Normative claim: `forkability` (and `surviveFounder`) in [`canonical.json`](./canonical.json).

## Explanatory notes

- Forking the specification, documentation, and reference implementations is intended.
- Economic claims, treasuries, ledgers, and legal ownership do **not** automatically copy on fork.
- Canonical Missionism is identified by **versioned** specification files in this repository (or a successor designated by a published change).

Intent: experimentation without capturing the governing organization, and without making the word “Missionism” meaningless.
