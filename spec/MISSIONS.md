# Missions

**Missionism Protocol v0.1 · Draft · September 2026**

Normative claims: `measurableMission`, `missionRecursion`, `missionAutonomy` in [`canonical.json`](./canonical.json).

## Structure (explanatory)

- Child missions may remain semi-autonomous while contributing upward.
- Parents evaluate whether children advance the larger goal; they do not automatically own the child’s product, hiring, or internal allocation.

## Open

- Exact value measurement methods
- How child missions participate in parent governance
- Federation representation formulas
