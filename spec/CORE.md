## Core idea

- Claim `purpose` — **why** Missionism exists.
- Claim `tripleAlignment` — a **test** that an organizational design is complete.

See [`canonical.json`](./canonical.json). Ownership is one tool. Mission accounting is another. Transparent prospective rules are another. Everything else is implementation.
