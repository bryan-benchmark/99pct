# Boundaries

A Mission must not:

- Violate **The Human Compact** (equal standing; nonviolence; reciprocity—non-forkable)  
- Violate the Globals  
- Hide harm or export costs invisibly  
- Let the same party redefine winning, certify success, and award itself ownership  
- Pretend speculative tokens are Proof of Mission progress  
- Claim Mishys (or any implementation) owns the definition of Missionism  
- Deny participation based on who someone is or what they peacefully believe, rather than behavior toward other humans  

Missionism remains usable without Mishys. That is a constitutional feature.
