# MCU Protocol

**MCU** = Mission Contribution Unit. An accounting mechanism for contribution — **not** a Global, **not** a share certificate.

**Companion:** [HUMAN_SUSTAINABILITY.md](./HUMAN_SUSTAINABILITY.md) — psychological guardrails for living with an immutable ledger. Make the ledger rigid; make the human experience forgiving. MCU is a receipt for serving the mission, never the mission itself.

## Principles

- Issue for proven contribution (and carefully attributed enabled contribution), not presence  
- Negative results / killed hypotheses can earn MCUs — information has value  
- Expanding denominator preferred to fixed pie theater  
- Cell/team pre-agrees splits before work when possible  
- Parent prices Proofs; MCU ≠ legal equity (mapping is a separate legal layer)  
- Never hard-code `1 MCU = X shares`  
- Every issuance should be explainable in one sentence (rule + contribution); see Human Sustainability  
- Never reduce a human to one universal MCU score or global leaderboard  
- MCU is **dual**: global contribution denomination + mission-local ownership claim ([ECONOMIC_PHILOSOPHY.md](./ECONOMIC_PHILOSOPHY.md))  
- **Pay locally. Own globally** — geography may change cash; it must not discount ownership credit for equal verified contribution  
- Collaboration allocations must be agreed **before** reward and published with the issuance  

## Immutability (what actually lasts)

Silent history rewrite must fail verification. Prefer: boring app DB + transparency log + Bitcoin/OpenTimestamps checkpoints + open verifier + many mirrors. MCU is not a chain asset. Detail: [TRANSPARENCY.md](./TRANSPARENCY.md).

## Mapping to ownership

MCUs measure contribution. **Contributor Shares** are the long-term economic (and eventually governance) mapping. **Capital Shares** let outside investors participate economically without buying the mission. One-way conversion only. Useful ownership—hold, windowed liquidity, conservative financing—is company-design doctrine, not an MCU feature. Full: `docs/OWNERSHIP_AND_CAPITAL_SYSTEM.md`. Spec: `OWNERSHIP.md`. Site: `/ownership`.

## Fork note

Forking a Mission copies reasoning and history. It does **not** automatically copy treasury, assets, or MCU economic claims.
