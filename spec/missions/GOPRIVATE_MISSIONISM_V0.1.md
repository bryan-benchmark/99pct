## Four corrections (locked)

1. **Optimize Builder Leverage, not headcount.** Goal = more mission value per fully loaded builder resource — not “fewer employees.” Understaffing, burnout, and hiding workers as contractors are failures, not wins.  
2. **50/50 is GoPrivate’s Mission Participation Split**, not a Missionism law. Other missions may choose different doer/builder splits.  
3. **The Mission Leader *office* is permanent. The human is not permanent by right.**  
4. **No buyout of service participation.** If the clinician leaves, future GoPrivate profit participation stops. Loans/capital are a separate contract. Already-earned shares stay theirs.

## Three different money systems (do not conflate)

### A. Clinician ↔ GoPrivate operating economics

Service Agreement (example baseline $300k):

| Slice | Split |
|-------|--------|
| First $300k qualifying owner earnings | **100% clinician** |
| Next $200k (example) | **50/50** while GoPrivate is the service provider |

If the clinician leaves:

- practice stays theirs  
- already-earned GoPrivate shares stay theirs  
- **future** GoPrivate profit participation **stops**  
- outstanding financing remains under the Financing Agreement  

Aligned with autonomy. Nothing permanent to “buy out.”

### B. GoPrivate Missionism equity mining

New company ownership continuously mined; allocated by Participation Split (50/50 for GoPrivate). Different from operating economics.

### C. Corporate dividends

No 50/50 rule. One economic share → proportional distribution.  
**Ownership was allocated at mining. Do not redistribute again when cash leaves.**

## Mission unit — QACY

### Qualified Autonomous Clinician Year

1 QACY per 12 qualifying months where all hold:

1. Autonomy materially above pre-GoPrivate baseline  
2. Owner earnings ≥ locked baseline  
3. Business viable and current on obligations  
4. Patient-safety / compliance gates pass  
5. Clinician retains real ability to leave (service terminable; no captivity)

Months accumulate fractionally.

Clinician Lane: equal Operator MCU weight per QACY (not per specialty dollars). Early clinicians get larger % naturally (smaller denominator) — no arbitrary early-adopter multiplier.

Clinicians may also earn **Builder Impact MCUs** by causing others’ QACY (mentorship, playbooks, etc.).

## Builder MCU types

| Type | Rule |
|------|------|
| **Base** | Grade × time for agreed useful work. **Not negotiable** by deal-making skill. Always for valid work. |
| **Risk** | Voluntary cash→MCU election; bounded (e.g. ≤25% of eligible cash; Risk MCU ≤1× Base). |
| **Impact** | Earned from verified extraordinary causation. No title ceiling. |

Package psychology: **Base = fair · Risk = my choice · Impact = my merit.**

## Builder Leverage (dashboard metric)

```text
Builder Leverage = Verified QACY created / fully loaded builder resources
```

Builder resources include employees, contractors, consultants, outsourced teams — otherwise people game “fewer employees.”

Status comes from mission accomplished per resource — not empire size.

### Hiring

**CEO decides hiring.** Existing builders do not vote on headcount.

Missionism surfaces whether a hire is **mission-accretive** (cash cost, expected Base MCU, incremental QACY, velocity effect, Builder-lane dilution, EV impact). Analogous to “is this acquisition EPS-accretive?”

Guard against under-hiring: maximize **sustainable** mission value per resource — with safety, quality, compliance, and worker sustainability constraints.

## GoPrivate government (one page)

### Economy

- Doers (clinicians): 50% newly mined Mission equity  
- Builders: 50%  
- Mission Leader: may earn Builder equity like any eligible builder  

### Government — three keys

| Key | Constituency |
|-----|----------------|
| **Doer House** | Clinicians / mission-production side |
| **Builder House** | People building the system |
| **Mission Leader** | Long-term mission |

**Any 2 of 3 may change future constitutional policy.**  
**Nobody may change the past.**

Mission Leader is the bridge when Doers and Builders disagree — **only on Reserved Mission Matters** (mission definition, equation, eras, participation split, constitutional changes, mission drift). Not product standups.

### Executive

**CEO runs the company** — hiring, firing, pricing, budgets, vendors, product, sales, org, normal compensation, capital deployment, execution. No referendum on ordinary ops.

### Clock

Slow 30y / fast 10y founding-block half-life via continuous Mission Velocity.

### Era rule

Current era: **immutable** once started.  
Next era: **must always exist** (auto-default carry-forward of equation, incremental target, split, MCU rules, capital policy). Proposed Era N+1 public ≥90 days before end; locks ≥30 days before if 2-of-3 approve; else default starts. No pause, no hostage.

### Employment rule

Useful work → Base. Bounded Risk → choice. Impact → merit. Firing stops future earning; does not rewrite past.

### Hiring rule

Maximize mission value per fully loaded builder resource — not minimize headcount.

### Exit rule

Leave service → future profit split stops. Keep practice. Keep earned equity. Debt is debt with transparent payoff/refinance.

## Founder special control (precise)

Two separate rights at start:

1. Founding economic shares  
2. Guaranteed Mission Leader seat as founder  

When founding block **&lt; 50% at era close** *or* **Year 30** (earlier):

- Economic shares **remain**  
- Guaranteed founder entitlement to Mission Leader seat **expires**  
- Person may still hold the office if governance continues to select/ratify them  

Not because “I filed papers in 2026.”

### Continuity

Mission Leader maintains **two ranked successors**. Death / resignation / incapacity / legal inability → Successor #1 becomes Acting Mission Leader. CEO keeps running; era and mining continue; no major constitutional change under acting status. Houses ratify permanent ML within ~180 days. Deadlock = status quo.

### Founder removal-for-cause (during privilege period)

Intentionally hard: **both Houses** + independent fiduciary finding that stated cause (fraud, incapacity, serious fiduciary abuse, criminal impossibility, asset theft attempts, etc.) is met. Not 51% of angry employees.

## What normal voting can and cannot do

After contributors hold real voting equity, they **can** elect directors, change leadership, pricing, service model, products, future compensation, even pivot the company. Feature — otherwise ownership is fake.

They **cannot** vote away the Immutable Kernel (see [CONSTITUTIONAL_LAYERS.md](../CONSTITUTIONAL_LAYERS.md)): stop mining forever, confiscate earned shares via Missionism, restart founder clock, erase past eras, close the door on future contributors.
