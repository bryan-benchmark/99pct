# Mishys — The Market for Missions

**Status:** Product doctrine. Mishys implements Missionism; it does not own Missionism.  
**Protocol escape hatch:** Anyone can run Missionism without Mishys.

Companions: [CONSTITUTION.md](./CONSTITUTION.md) · [CERTIFICATION.md](./CERTIFICATION.md) · [DECISION_COMPILER.md](./DECISION_COMPILER.md)

## Framing

> **Mishys is the market for missions.**  
> Companies publish what they are trying to accomplish, how they measure it, what ownership remains to be mined, and what they need next.  
> Humans decide where their next unit of effort has the highest personal and mission return.

| Market | Allocates |
|--------|-----------|
| Capital markets | Capital |
| Labor markets (Indeed, etc.) | Labor titles |
| **Mishys** | **Human agency toward missions** |

Indeed asks: “What jobs are available?”  

Mishys asks:

> **Where would a person like me create the most useful value right now—and what would I receive if I did?**

Eventually the inverse:

> **There is no existing mission solving this well. Should you start one?**

## Sovereignty vs discovery

Every Missionism company remains sovereign.

- Benchmark owns `benchmark.care/mission` (+ `/mission.json`)  
- GoPrivate owns `goprivate.care/mission`  
- NextBed owns its mission record  

**Mishys is not the source of truth.**

It **reads, verifies, archives, compares, and makes sense of** the source of truth.

Company: “Here is our mission.”  
Mishys: “Here is what that means relative to every other mission.”

Protocol stays decentralized. Discovery is centralized enough to be useful.

## Primary object: Need (not Job)

Traditional post:

> Senior Full-Stack Engineer · $180K–$220K

Missionism Need:

```text
Current constraint: Simulation creation requires 7.8 expert hours per case.
Era target:         <1 hour.
Mission consequence: Est. +3,400 Excellent Worker-Years/year if solved.
Skills:             LLM systems, instructional design, workflow automation…
Cash range:         $180K–$220K
Base ownership:     X
Impact opportunity: I3–I5 depending on verified result
Mineable Founder Units this Era: 3.8
```

Applicant posture: **“I think I can solve that.”** — not “I have that title.”

### Need types (resource-allocation market)

Employment · Contract · Mission bounty · Research experiment · Creator partnership · Founder opportunity · Vendor · Capital · Acquisition · Open-source contribution

Not every Need is “become our employee.”

## Reverse recruiting: constraint graph match

User states capabilities, cash needs, risk tolerance, values (e.g. healthcare access, clinician autonomy).

Mishys searches **constraints across missions**, not titles.

Example outputs: GoPrivate 96% (payer enrollment 110 days) · NextBed 89% (phone coordination 37%) · Benchmark 71% (eval reliability less direct fit).

An entirely different labor market.

## Ownership Mine as market signal

Not just “valuation $80M.”

```text
Founder Reserve remaining:     71.2 units
Contributor-owned:             28.8%
Mineable this Era:             4.0 units
Already mined this Era:        1.1
Mission acceleration potential:+2.0 units
Era remaining:                 213 days
Eligible contributors:         46
```

Early transition ≠ always better; mature company may be safer/more valuable. Make the trade visible.

## No single “best company” score

Dumb ranking is immediately gameable. Expose transparent dimensions; **user chooses weights**.

| Dimension | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Mission Alignment** | Care about stated outcome |
| **Need Fit** | Capabilities vs current constraint |
| **Marginal Impact Potential** | Solving this constraint → Mission Equation move |
| **Ownership Opportunity** | Reserve, release, rules, impact band |
| **Cash Fit** | Meets compensation requirements |
| **Evidence Quality** | Confidence Mission Equation measures reality |
| **Mission Velocity** | Arrows actually moving |
| **Human Burden** | Exposure / lifestyle |
| **Company Health** | Runway, profitability, resilience |
| **Liquidity** | Dividends, tenders, secondaries, public, or none |
| **Freedom** | Restrictions, vesting, switching, portability |
| **Certification Confidence** | How much of the record is independently verified |

Ambitious 24-year-old: ownership 35%, impact 30%, cash 10%.  
Parent of three: cash 30%, stability 25%, burden 20%.  

Neither wrong. **Mishys finds the best positive-sum trade for that human.**

## Company-side talent tool

Mishys can warn: **unlikely to hit Era** — dominant constraint is credentialing, but team is 23 engineers / 8 sales / 1 ops expert.

> Hiring another frontend engineer: low expected Mission ROI.  
> Highest expected need: payer-enrollment systems operator.

The mission creates the posting. Harder to fake than executive headcount theater.

## Trust gradient (certification)

| Layer | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Self-reported | Company claims this |
| Missionism Committed | Rules satisfy standard prospectively |
| Missionism Certified | Historical Era records verified |
| Independently evidenced | Key outcomes have external evidence |

Startups aren’t punished for lack of history — history is simply shown as absent.

## Mishys flywheel

```text
More Missionism companies publish structured data
  → better map of problems, outcomes, skills
  → humans choose better where to work
  → talent reaches highest marginal mission value
  → constraints solve faster → contributors earn more
  → Missionism attracts exceptional workers
  → more companies adopt Missionism
  → more structured data → map improves
```

Societal side: scarce ability allocated toward places that appear capable of producing greater value.

## Existential integrity rule

> **Do not let companies pay Mishys to rank higher.**

Ranking cannot secretly be an ad auction.

Revenue (later): certification, recruiting infrastructure, talent matching, mission analytics, company tools, verified data, capital matching, transaction services — **not pay-to-rank on mission fit.**

If people decide years of their lives here, ranking integrity is existential.
