## Shared architecture (locked insight)

AI collapses the cost of converting unstructured human thought into structured output. Value migrates toward what intelligence alone cannot manufacture:

**Experience + Truth + Judgment + Trust + Action + Coordination + Accountability**

Three questions, one architecture:

| Domain | Question |
|--------|----------|
| **Missionism** | How do we turn human desire to improve something into coordinated action with maximum long-term value? |
| **Benchmark** | How do we turn human experience into transferable competency with maximum long-term value? |
| **AI use** | How do I turn messy thinking into better decisions with maximum long-term value? |

Pipeline (same shape everywhere):

```
messy human signal → structure → truth/grounding → decision → action → measurement → learning → compounding knowledge
```

**“Benchmark it”** names that transformation: something happened → extract what’s worth learning → determine what’s true → make wisdom reusable.

### Highest-ROI product goal (Benchmark lab)

\[
\frac{\text{Maximum useful human wisdom captured and transferred}}{\text{Minimum human effort + error}}
\]

Optimize **that** equation—not “quality of AI-generated simulations.”

## Experience Leverage (Benchmark)

**Before AI** — production was the bottleneck:

\[
\text{Training Value} = \frac{\text{Quality} \times \text{Relevance} \times \text{Learners Reached}}{\text{SME Time} \times \text{ID Time} \times \text{Production} \times \text{Maintenance}}
\]

Old optimization: SME → Creation Process → Training. Build a better builder (Articulate, templates, libraries).

**After AI** — denominator terms (formatting, ID translation, scenario/rubric drafting, branching, production) collapse toward zero. Value leaves the builder.

New chain:

```
Human Experience → AI Interpretation → Trusted Standard → Practice → Evidence → Organizational Learning
```

Optimize: **How much trustworthy learning can we extract from each unit of human experience?**

\[
\textbf{Experience Leverage} = \frac{\text{Truth Captured} \times \text{People Improved} \times \text{Behavior Improvement} \times \text{Future Reuse}}{\text{Expert Effort} \times \text{Time} \times \text{Friction} \times \text{Interpretation Error}}
\]

**Sorter implication:** 90 seconds of expert speech → Moment → competency → Strong/Partial/Miss → policy gaps → one consequential question → approved Challenge → learners at scale → competency data. Numerator explodes; denominator collapses. That’s the business.

**Empirical honesty:** Current studies mostly show ~10–50% productivity gains. Order-of-magnitude (≈100×) opportunities appear when the **unit of production** changes—not when workers merely get faster at the old unit. Benchmark’s discovery is one such unit change (training produced / production cost → wisdom captured × people × reuse / expert effort + error). Five larger equation shifts sit above or beside it.

## Five larger AI-era equation shifts (horizon map)

Search principle: find places where **extremely valuable human/world signal** exists, but most value is lost because converting it into coordinated action requires **expensive human translation**.

Generalized equation (carry this forward):

\[
\textbf{Value} = \frac{\textbf{Quality of human/world signal} \times \textbf{Correct action} \times \textbf{Scale} \times \textbf{Compounding}}{\textbf{Human translation effort} \times \textbf{Friction} \times \textbf{Delay} \times \textbf{Error}}
\]

Pre-AI world pays humans to translate: experience→training, question→experiment, patient→docs→decision, knowledge→teaching, expertise→novice, mission→tasks→people→coordination. AI collapses those layers.

| High-value, low-structure signal | Domain |
|----------------------------------|--------|
| “I remember this difficult patient…” | Benchmark (found) |
| “I wonder why…” | Science |
| “Something feels wrong with this patient…” | Healthcare |
| “I don’t understand…” | Education |
| “Here’s how I learned to solve this…” | Expertise distribution |
| “Somebody should fix this…” | Missionism |

### Ranking by potential equation shift

| Rank | Domain | Old scarce resource | AI-era scarce resource | Potential | Proven-ness |
|------|--------|---------------------|------------------------|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | Scientific discovery | Scientist experimentation cycles | Physical reality + good questions | ★★★★★ | Emerging (self-driving labs, AI-discovered drugs in clinic) |
| 2 | Healthcare | Clinician labor | Trusted judgment + intervention capacity | ★★★★★ | Early (ambient AI stage one) |
| 3 | Education | Teacher attention | Motivation + validated mastery | ★★★★★ | Early RCTs (e.g. AI tutor gains); not universal proof |
| 4 | Expertise distribution | Experienced workers | Authoritative judgment + feedback | ★★★★☆ | Stronger early field evidence (support AI, P&G) |
| 5 | Mission coordination | Management / coordination capacity | Real commitment + resources + measurable outcomes | ★★★★★ | Least proven; deepest Missionism bet |

### 1. Science — experiments performed → uncertainty eliminated

**Old:** Useful discoveries / (scientists × experiment time × lab cost × failed experiments). Bottleneck = serial human experimental loop.

**New:**

\[
\text{Discovery Value} = \frac{\text{Important uncertainty eliminated} \times P(\text{discovery matters})}{\text{physical resources} \times \text{elapsed time} \times \text{human oversight}}
\]

AI joins: hypothesis → experiment selection → robotic execution → measurement → interpretation → next experiment (continuously). Self-driving labs + multi-agent campaign orchestration; AI-discovered assets entering human trials.

**Stop maximizing experiments completed. Maximize important uncertainty destroyed per dollar and day.**

Why ≫ Benchmark scale: one expert memory → ~10k learners vs one scientific objective → millions of machine-selected experiments → material/drug/process → potentially billions affected.

### 2. Healthcare — clinician productivity → healthy life created per clinician-minute

**Old:** Visits/procedures/tasks / (clinician hours + cost) — wrong numerator and often wrong denominator.

**New:**

\[
\text{Healthcare Value} = \frac{\Delta\text{Healthy Life} \times P(\text{successful outcome}) \times \text{patient preference}}{\text{clinician time} \times \text{total cost} \times \text{patient burden} \times \text{risk}}
\]

AI’s job is not “type faster.” Move every scarce clinician-minute to highest expected patient value.

100× path: story captured → structured history → missing info sought → evidence retrieved → risks prioritized → options simulated → preference incorporated → clinician judgment → follow-up automated → outcome observed → system learns.

Humans concentrate on: **judgment + trust + physical intervention + accountability + compassion.** Ambient scribes = stage one (burnout/docs relief); not the end state. Expands effective healthcare supply.

### 3. Education — instruction delivered → mastery created

Civilization-scale form of Benchmark’s insight.

**Old:** Curriculum delivered / (teachers × class time) → 30 kids + 1 teacher + one speed.

**New:**

\[
\text{Learning Value} = \frac{\Delta\text{Mastery} \times \text{retention} \times \text{transfer} \times \text{motivation}}{\text{learner time} \times \text{teacher time} \times \text{cost}}
\]

100× is not “AI teaches 2× better.” It is: one exceptional pedagogy → individualized instruction to ~100M learners at near-zero marginal instructional labor → breaks \(\text{students reached} \propto \text{teachers available}\).

### 4. Work — hiring expertise → distributing expertise

**Old:** Organizational capability ≈ Σ employee expertise → hire / train / hope they stay.

**New:**

\[
\text{Organizational Capability} = \frac{\text{best available judgment} \times \text{\# decisions reached} \times \text{learning from outcomes}}{\text{expert attention required per decision}}
\]

Early evidence: gen-AI ~14% overall / ~34% for novices in support (best-worker behaviors transmitted); P&G RCT — individual+AI matched two-person team without AI on product problems.

Before: 1 expert : maybe 5–20 apprentices. After: 1 expert’s validated judgment → potentially every worker.

**Benchmark is a special case:** preserve a great nurse’s difficult moments → extend to how best performers perceive, decide, prioritize, recover from exceptions → available at the moment of need.

**Future KPI:** % of consequential decisions that have access to best available expertise (not “how many experts”).

### 5. Organizations / Missionism — coordinating people → maximizing verified progress

Least empirically mature; potentially largest. Traditional org = technology for coordination. Enormous waste in finding collaborators, context, meetings, duplication, misalignment, grants, procurement, “who should do what,” “did it work.”

**Old:** Mission Progress ≈ (People × Capital × Effort) / (coordination cost + information loss + misalignment).

**New:**

\[
\text{Mission Value} = \frac{\text{verified progress toward outcome} \times P(\text{progress matters}) \times \text{future compounding}}{\text{money} \times \text{human effort} \times \text{time} \times \text{risk}}
\]

Imagine “Reduce mosquito-borne illness in Atlanta” without forming a company first: AI decomposes goal → finds work → bottlenecks → experiments → capable people → ROI → allocates → tracks contribution → measures → replans → rewards. Humans where comparative advantage is largest; AI as connective tissue.

If it works, scarcity becomes **valuable missions + human commitment + real-world resources**, not organizations capable of coordinating them — deepest Missionism.

**Build implication:** Phase 0 still ships Benchmark-shaped activation. Horizon bets (science uncertainty, healthy-life/clinician-min, mastery@scale, expertise distribution KPI, Mission Value equation) inform Mission Graph, matching, and MCU later—do not block the &lt;10 min loop.

\[
\text{Value from AI} \approx \frac{\text{Answer Quality}}{\text{Prompt Effort}}

\[
\text{AI Insight Value} = \frac{\text{Important Assumptions Challenged} \times \text{Novel Frames} \times \text{Decision Impact} \times \text{P(right)}}{\text{Time} \times \text{Cognitive Effort} \times \text{Noise}}

## Mission-aligned pricing (Benchmark capitalism)

Full doctrine + examples: **`docs/MISSION_ALIGNED_PRICING.md`**. Index: **`docs/PRINCIPLES.md`**.

**Top sentence:** Mission determines what we subsidize. Value determines what we charge.

**Not:** “Mission means cheap” (philanthropy in SaaS clothes).  
**Not:** Cost-plus (“voice $0.70 → charge $1”). Transparent ≠ cost-plus; capture share of value created.

**Move the toll booth:** Want practice → evidence → improvement → validated capability abundant. Don’t put friction on every Challenge seat. Charge for competency **infrastructure** (standards, measurement, evidence retention, alerts, remediation, calibration, compliance, analytics, integration, governance, service).

**Platform pattern:** Subsidize the side you want to grow (Google search, GitHub developers, Canva individuals). Abundance raises the value of the paid layer.

```
Free practice → more attempts → better competency infrastructure
  → better enterprise product → enterprise revenue
  → better Benchmark → more free practice
```

Mission causes the business model to compound—it doesn’t sit beside it.

**Architecture:** Abundance free/cheap · Coordination / Customization / Trust / Governance / Service / Enterprise infrastructure paid. Never make the mission more expensive simply because it is succeeding.

**Price discriminate:** Individual free → educator cheap → small org affordable → large org substantial → humans/governance/integration more. Do **not** undercharge enterprise “for the mission”—that surplus funds access for rural nurse / student / small clinic.

**Incentive alignment (better than seat SaaS):** CFO approves infrastructure contract; trainers say “use the hell out of it.” Customer succeeds → unit economics improve for both; employee #4827 doesn’t hit a license wall.

### Pricing track (when packaging exists)

| Priority | Work |
|----------|------|
| P0 | Encode doctrine in packaging: free practice / contribute vs paid enterprise infrastructure |
| P1 | Kill per-practice mission-throttling tolls as default |
| P2 | Segment ladder (individual → educator → SMB → enterprise → service/governance) |
| P3 | Instrument flywheel metrics: public attempts → scoring quality → enterprise conversion → reinvestment |
| P4 | Never pitch cost-plus; pitch value captured vs trainer labor / competency risk |

## Useful ownership (constitutional, not yet product)

Full doctrine: **`docs/OWNERSHIP_AND_CAPITAL_SYSTEM.md`**. Promise: **`docs/OWNERSHIP_THAT_CHANGES_LIVES.md`**. Spec: **`spec/OWNERSHIP.md`**. Site: **`/ownership`**. Meta-metric: **`/meta`**.

**Top sentence:** A paycheck pays for today. Ownership should help pay for tomorrow.

```
SALARY + OWNERSHIP + USEFUL FINANCIAL TOOLS = MORE LIFETIME FREEDOM
```

**Four clocks:** Today = salary · This year = bonus · Decades = Contributor Shares · Generations = mission governance.

**Share classes:** Contributor Shares (employee economics + governance) · Capital Shares (outside capital without buying the mission) · Mission Constitution (cannot be purchased). One-way path Contributor → Capital. Private is the destination; public is the escape hatch. Never treat IPO as graduation.

**Capital stack:** retained profits first when cash flow is strong · debt when it can be serviced · Capital Shares for risk capital · public equity last. Accept a governance discount if smaller than protected mission control.

**Useful, not trapped:** hold and compound · sell in organized windows · conservative financing against eligible vested shares (home first; small Life Buffer). Partner with regulated institutions before building a bank.

**Anti-company-town:** vested ownership stays the employee’s; leaving does not call ordinary loans due; lenders never acquire mission governance; employees may use outside lenders; financial data consent-based and separated from employment; no unlimited repurchase promise; wealth inheritable, permanent political control not automatically inherited.

**Recession design:** no ordinary mark-to-market margin loans for core life needs; several liquidity sources; cap company repurchases as a share of cash/FCF; stress-test 50–70% declines plus simultaneous demand. Public listing does not automatically make employee borrowing safer.

**Do not ship this as product yet.** Lending, liquidity windows, valuation infrastructure, and capital markets are Phase 7. Until the activation loop works, this is doctrine for company design—not a Mishys feature.
